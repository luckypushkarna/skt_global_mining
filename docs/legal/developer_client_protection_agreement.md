## PART 1: DEVELOPER–CLIENT AGREEMENT CLAUSES

### 1. CLIENT-SUPPLIED MATERIALS
"Client Materials" shall mean all content supplied, selected, requested, approved, or directed by the Client for use in the project, including but not limited to:
- photographs
- videos
- logos
- trademarks
- written copy
- documents
- illustrations
- icons
- fonts
- audio
- animations
- location information
- employee photographs
- partner branding
- third-party links
- media downloaded or supplied from external sources

The Client is solely responsible for reviewing and approving all Client Materials prior to deployment.

### 2. CLIENT OWNERSHIP AND LICENSING WARRANTY
The Client represents and warrants that:
- It owns the Client Materials or possesses valid permission and/or licences to use them.
- The licences permit commercial website use.
- The licences permit the required editing, resizing, hosting, and public display of the materials.
- All required creator permissions, model releases, property releases, and trademark approvals have been obtained.
- The Client Materials do not knowingly violate copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, confidentiality, or other third-party rights.
- All information supplied is lawful and factually authorised for publication.
- Any required attribution instructions have been provided to the Developer in writing.

### 3. DEVELOPER’S LIMITED ROLE
The parties acknowledge and agree that:
- The Developer provides technical design, development, integration, and deployment services.
- The Developer is not the original publisher or rights holder of the Client Materials.
- Unless separately agreed in writing, the Developer is not engaged to conduct a legal title search or independently verify every asset licence.
- The Developer may reasonably rely on the Client’s written ownership and permission confirmations.
- The Developer’s technical inclusion of Client Materials does not constitute a legal opinion regarding ownership.
- The Client retains final editorial and publication control over the project.

### 4. PRE-DEPLOYMENT CONTENT APPROVAL
Before production deployment, the Client must approve in writing all visible text, images, videos, logos, partner branding, legal entity details, contact information, legal pages, third-party integrations, the asset-rights register, and the final staging website. Deployment shall not proceed while material ownership questions remain unresolved.

### 5. CLIENT INDEMNITY
To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Client agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold the Developer harmless from any third-party claims, losses, damages, reasonable legal costs, and settlements arising directly from:
- The Client Materials.
- Inaccurate client ownership representations.
- Missing licences or permissions.
- Client instructions regarding the inclusion of specific content.
- Trademark or copyright complaints involving the Client Materials.
- Privacy or publicity claims involving client-supplied photographs or information.
- Content changed or uploaded by the Client after handover.

**Procedural Protections:**
- The Developer must notify the Client promptly after receiving a claim.
- The Client may control the defence with competent counsel.
- The Developer must provide reasonable cooperation.
- The Client may not enter a settlement imposing liability or admission on the Developer without the Developer's written consent.
- Reasonable cooperation costs incurred by the Developer shall be covered by the Client.

This indemnity obligation shall survive project completion and termination where legally permitted.

### 6. FAIR EXCLUSIONS
The Client indemnity shall not apply to the extent a claim was caused by:
- Content independently sourced and added by the Developer without Client instruction or approval.
- The Developer using an asset outside the scope authorised by the Client.
- Unauthorised Developer modifications that create the infringement.
- The Developer knowingly ignoring a confirmed infringement.
- Fraud, wilful misconduct, or gross negligence by the Developer.
- Breach of confidentiality or data-security duties caused by the Developer.

### 7. DEVELOPER RIGHT TO REFUSE OR REMOVE CONTENT
The Developer reserves the right to:
- Refuse to publish suspicious or clearly unlawful material.
- Request proof of ownership or licensing.
- Temporarily disable disputed content.
- Remove content following a credible rights complaint.
- Pause deployment until the Client provides clarification.
- Recommend a licensed replacement.

Exercising this right shall not make the Developer responsible for verifying every other Client Material.

### 8. POST-HANDOVER RESPONSIBILITY
After final handover, the Client assumes full responsibility for:
- New uploads and content changes.
- Renewed or expired licences.
- Ongoing legal-page accuracy.
- Cookie and third-party integration changes.
- Responding to copyright and privacy requests.
- Maintaining proof of licences.
- Security and maintenance, unless covered by a separate written agreement.

The Developer remains responsible for post-handover work only when included in an active written maintenance agreement.

Subject to applicable law:
- Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential losses except where such exclusion is unlawful.
- The Developer’s aggregate contractual liability shall be capped at [LIABILITY CAP — CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL].
- This cap shall not exclude liabilities that cannot legally be limited.

### D. TAKEDOWN-RESPONSE WORKFLOW

In the event of a copyright, privacy, or trademark complaint received post-deployment:

1. **Receipt & Notification:** Whichever party receives the complaint must notify the other party promptly.
2. **Temporary Disablement:** The Developer (if under an active maintenance contract) or the Client will temporarily disable or remove the disputed content pending review.
3. **Rights Verification:** The Client will review their Asset Rights Register and licensing documentation to determine the validity of the complaint.
4. **Resolution:** 
   - If the claim is invalid, the Client will respond to the complainant with proof of rights (with counsel's guidance).
   - If the claim is valid, the Client will instruct the Developer to permanently replace or remove the material.
5. **Indemnity Activation:** If the claim escalates to legal action or demands for damages regarding Client Materials, the Developer will formally invoke the Client Indemnity clause, handing defense control to the Client's competent counsel while providing reasonable cooperation.
