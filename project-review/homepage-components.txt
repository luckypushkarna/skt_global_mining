Homepage Component Structure (Render Order)
===========================================

1. HeroSection
   - Source: src/components/organisms/HeroSection.tsx
   - Client/Server: Client Component (use client)
   - Animation: GSAP / Framer Motion
   - ScrollTrigger: Yes
   - Media: Hero video/background
   - Lazy-loading: Eager loaded (LCP)
   - Performance Concerns: High resolution media could delay LCP if not optimized.

2. AboutSection
   - Source: src/components/organisms/AboutSection.tsx
   - Client/Server: Client Component
   - Lazy-loading: Wrapped in Suspense

3. BlankSection
   - Source: src/components/organisms/BlankSection.tsx
   - Client/Server: Client Component
   - Lazy-loading: Wrapped in Suspense

4. ServicesSection
   - Source: src/components/organisms/ServicesSection.tsx
   - Client/Server: Client Component
   - Lazy-loading: Wrapped in Suspense

5. OperationalScaleSection
   - Source: src/components/organisms/OperationalScaleSection.tsx
   - Client/Server: Client Component
   - ScrollTrigger: Yes
   - Media: Maps / Data visualization
   - Lazy-loading: Wrapped in Suspense

6. StatsSection
   - Source: src/components/organisms/StatsSection.tsx
   - Client/Server: Client Component
   - Animation: Live Counters
   - Lazy-loading: Wrapped in Suspense

7. ChairmanMessageSection

8. GallerySection

9. TeamSection

10. CommunityImpactSection
